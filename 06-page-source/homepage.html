ALMA MATER MAH JONGG

Where Game Night Meets School Pride

Alma Mater Mah Jongg was created for people who carry their school pride with them long after graduation. Our collections bring together the timeless game of Mah Jongg with the traditions and identity of beloved universities. Each tile is thoughtfully designed to reflect the colors, icons, and spirit that make every campus special.

Whether you’re hosting a weekly game night, reconnecting with old friends, or introducing new players to the game, Alma Mater Mah Jongg creates a meaningful way to celebrate the places that shaped us.

THE STAMP OF APPROVAL

Officially Licensed Collegiate Designs

Every Alma Mater Mah Jongg collection is produced under official licensing agreements with the universities we represent.

Each design is reviewed and approved by the institution, ensuring authenticity and quality you can trust. When you purchase an officially licensed product, you’re also supporting the programs and traditions that make these schools special.
